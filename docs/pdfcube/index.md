PDFCube is a PDF presentation viewer with animated transitions, automatic
slide timing and keyboard-controlled zoom. It brings cube rotations, page
flips, shattering glass and smooth fades to slide decks from LaTeX Beamer and
office suites.

It was born in a few sleepless nights before graduation in 2006 and, despite
the horrible style it was written in, earned its authors some popularity and
free beers. After a long time self-hosted on Subversion and Trac it moved to
GitHub.

## Twenty years of spinning cubes

The first line of PDFCube was committed in 2006.

For its twentieth birthday PDFCube was completely rewritten and in October 2026
moved in six days and eighteen releases to 0.9.2. It now ships every PDF
transition, from blinds and page flips to shattering glass. The cube is still
the star. It now runs on Linux, macOS and Windows, and it is still loved
by LaTeX Beamer users, who finally have a reason to set slide transitions.
